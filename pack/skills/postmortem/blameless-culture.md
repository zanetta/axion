# Blameless Culture

| Blame-focused | Blameless |
|---------------|----------|
| Who caused this? | What conditions allowed this? |
| Someone made a mistake | The system allowed the mistake |
| Punish individuals | Improve systems and processes |
| Hide information | Share learnings |
| Fear of speaking up | Psychological safety |

## Facilitator stance

- Thank people who surface near-misses and incomplete data.
- Separate **accountability** (own the fix) from **punishment**.
- Prefer “we” language for system gaps; name teams for action ownership, not guilt.

## Why it matters

Without safety, timelines lie, contributing factors stay hidden, and the next incident repeats the same path.
